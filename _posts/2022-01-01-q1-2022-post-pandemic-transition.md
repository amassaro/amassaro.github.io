2022 begins with the world attempting to return to some version of normal.

## Transitional Period

After two years of pandemic-driven remote work, there's a lot of discussion about what comes next. Hybrid work, return to office, permanently remote - every organization is figuring out their approach.

## Steady Work

Project work continues at a steady pace. The frenetic energy of the early pandemic has settled into more sustainable rhythms.

## BigBlueButton Maintenance

The video conferencing work from 2020-2021 continues in maintenance mode. The platform is stable and deployed, requiring only occasional updates.
