## Ongoing Projects

Software development isn't always about building new things. Much of the work is maintaining, improving, and supporting existing systems. There's satisfaction in keeping things running smoothly.

My technology stack has stabilized over the past few years. PHP with Laravel for web backends, TypeScript for frontends, occasional forays into other languages for specific needs. There's value in knowing your tools deeply.
