Spring brings continued refinement and new capabilities to the kiosk platform.

## Platform Consolidation

The various components I've been building over the past year - the application designer, rules engine, model editor, and feed data system - are coming together into a more cohesive platform. It's satisfying to see disparate pieces start working as a unified whole.

## Activity Tracking

New this quarter is activity tracking and logging infrastructure. Understanding how users interact with kiosk applications is crucial for optimization. What screens do they spend time on? Where do they drop off? This data drives improvements.

## Architecture Lessons

One thing I've learned building this platform: start simple but plan for complexity. The initial architecture decisions made a year ago are now paying dividends as we add features. Conversely, a few shortcuts taken early on are now causing headaches.
