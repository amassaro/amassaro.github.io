As 2025 approaches its end, it's clear this has been an exceptional year.

## Record Contributions

With 699 contributions already and the year not over, 2025 is my highest-activity year on GitHub. That's more than the early pandemic years, more than any year before. The combination of interesting projects, established tools, and focused work time has created unprecedented productivity.

## Continued Momentum

All the major projects continue: onboarding forms, booking systems, e-commerce, Python APIs, scientific computing. The portfolio of active work is broader than ever.

## Reflections on a Decade

Looking back to that last blog post in August 2015, it's remarkable how much has changed. From PHP and Go experiments to C# enterprise tools to TypeScript frontends to Rust learning to Python APIs to Mathematica scientific computing. The thread connecting it all is continuous learning and building.

## What's Next?

The future is always uncertain, but the foundations are strong. A decade of consistent contribution has built skills, tools, and patterns that make new projects easier. Whatever comes next, the preparation is there.
