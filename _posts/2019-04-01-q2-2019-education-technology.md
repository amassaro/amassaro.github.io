Spring brings work in the education space.

## Tutoring Platforms

I've been working on a tutoring club website. Education technology is an interesting space - you're building tools that help people learn. The UX considerations are different from typical business software. You want to minimize friction and maximize engagement.

## CSS and Styling

This project has been a good excuse to focus on CSS and frontend styling. It's easy to get caught up in backend architecture and forget that the visual presentation matters enormously to users.

## Industry Shift

After years of heavy C# work, my focus has been shifting more toward PHP and web technologies. The virtual landing platform, the tutoring site, and various side projects are all web-based. It's a natural evolution as more software moves to the browser.
