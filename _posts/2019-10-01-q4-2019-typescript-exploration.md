The end of 2019 brings deeper exploration of TypeScript.

## TypeScript Projects

I've been working on a TypeScript-based project called Situs. TypeScript has become increasingly compelling - it brings the type safety I appreciate from C# to the JavaScript world. The tooling and IDE support have matured significantly.

## Virtual Landing Updates

The virtual landing basic platform continues to receive updates. It's become a reliable workhorse for generating simple landing pages quickly.

## Year in Review

2019 saw a clear shift in my technology focus. The year started with PHP and Twilio, moved through education technology, and ended with TypeScript. The booking platform started this year will continue to be a focus going forward. Looking back, it's been a year of building practical, useful tools rather than exploring cutting-edge technology.
