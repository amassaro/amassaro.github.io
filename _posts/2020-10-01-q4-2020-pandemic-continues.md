As 2020 winds down, remote work remains the norm.

## Maintenance Mode

Many projects this quarter have been in maintenance mode. The urgency of Q2 has given way to steady, sustainable work. Bug fixes, small improvements, keeping things running.

## Year in Review

2020 was defined by the pandemic. From a technology perspective, it was the year of video conferencing, remote collaboration, and rapid adaptation. My GitHub activity was higher than usual - 174 total contributions, driven largely by the BigBlueButton work.

The year taught us that technology can adapt quickly when it needs to. It also highlighted the importance of robust, self-hostable open-source solutions.
