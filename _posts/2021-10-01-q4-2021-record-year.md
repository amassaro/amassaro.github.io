As 2021 closes, it's clear this has been an exceptional year for GitHub activity.

## Year in Review

434 total contributions - my highest year ever. The pandemic's second year turned out to be incredibly productive from a coding perspective. More time at home, established remote work patterns, and interesting projects all contributed.

## BigBlueButton Updates

The BigBlueButton fork continues to receive updates. Video conferencing remains important even as some people return to offices.

## Looking Ahead

With vaccines rolling out and the world slowly reopening, 2022 might look different. But the productivity habits developed during the pandemic - focused work time, async communication, side projects - are worth keeping.
