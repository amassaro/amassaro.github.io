Spring 2021 is proving to be a highly productive period.

## Increased Contributions

My GitHub activity is at an all-time high. The combination of continued remote work and interesting projects has led to more coding than ever. Sometimes the best thing for productivity is simply having uninterrupted time.

## Various Projects

Work continues across multiple fronts - marketing tools, video conferencing improvements, and internal projects. The variety keeps things interesting.

## Remote Work Culture

By now, remote work has evolved from emergency measure to established practice. Teams have figured out asynchronous communication, video meeting etiquette, and the importance of documentation. It's a different way of working, but it works.
