As 2022 ends, it's time for reflection.

## Year in Review

390 contributions - another strong year, though slightly below 2021's peak. The NodeGUI exploration was the most notable new direction, but most of the year was spent on incremental improvements to existing projects.

## Technology Trends

The JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem continues to evolve rapidly. New frameworks appear constantly, but I've found value in sticking with proven tools and learning them deeply rather than chasing every new thing.

## The Value of Consistency

Looking back over the past few years, consistency has been more valuable than intensity. Regular, steady contributions add up over time. The projects I've maintained for years are more valuable than the ones I built quickly and abandoned.
