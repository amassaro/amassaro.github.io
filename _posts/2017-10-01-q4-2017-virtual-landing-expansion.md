As 2017 wraps up, the virtual landing page concept introduced last year is getting serious attention.

## Dynamic Landing Pages

The virtual landing system has evolved significantly. What started as a simple concept for generating landing pages has grown into a more sophisticated platform. The system can now create customized experiences based on various parameters - source, campaign, user attributes, and more.

## Kiosk Platform Updates

The kiosk platform received its final major updates for the year. It's reached a mature state where it's being actively used in production. There's something satisfying about seeing software you built being used by real people to solve real problems.

## Year in Review

2017 has been about taking the foundations built in 2016 and turning them into production-ready systems. The kiosk platform, case management system, and virtual landing tools all represent significant engineering effort that's now delivering value.
