Summer brings work on scheduling and booking functionality.

## Booking Platform Development

I've started work on a booking system - the kind of software that lets businesses manage appointments, reservations, and schedules. It's a crowded space but there's always room for solutions tailored to specific needs.

## Laravel and Blade

The booking platform uses Laravel with Blade templates. After years of working with various PHP frameworks, Laravel has become my go-to. The ecosystem is mature, the documentation is excellent, and it strikes a good balance between convention and flexibility.

## Common Patterns

Booking systems share patterns with other business software: user management, calendar handling, notifications, payments. What makes each one unique is the specific workflow and business rules layered on top.
