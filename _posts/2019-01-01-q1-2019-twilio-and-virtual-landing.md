The new year starts with telephony integration and continued platform development.

## Simple Phone Answering Service

I built a simple answering service using Twilio. When you receive a call, it sends you an email with the details. It's a straightforward application but incredibly useful - perfect for situations where you can't answer but want to know who called.

## Silex Twilio Provider Updates

This work also prompted updates to my old Silex Twilio Provider library. Even though Silex is essentially end-of-life (superseded by Symfony), the provider still works and still has users.

## Virtual Landing Continues

The PHP-based virtual landing platform from Q4 2018 continues to evolve. This quarter brought refinements to the admin interface and improved page rendering performance.

## A Simplified Approach

I also created a "basic" version of virtual landing - stripped down to essentials for simpler use cases. Sometimes less is more.
