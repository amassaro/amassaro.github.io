The first quarter of 2020 started normally enough, though we all know what was coming.

## Virtual Landing Improvements

The virtual landing basic platform received continued attention. Small improvements and bug fixes - the kind of steady maintenance that keeps software running smoothly.

## Business as Usual

January and February felt like any other start to a year. Existing projects continued. Plans were made. By March, everything changed.

## Looking Back

It's hard to write about Q1 2020 without acknowledging what came next. At the time, we were just doing our work. In retrospect, it was the last "normal" quarter for a while.
